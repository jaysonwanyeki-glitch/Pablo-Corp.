Our Process

From plant to pastry,
nothing is left to chance

We work with a single regional farm, infuse in-house, and test every batch twice. Here's how a cookie reaches you.

01

Single-source flower

Sun-grown, hang-dried and hand-trimmed by a farm we've worked with for six years.

02

Slow infusion

Low-and-decaboxylation, then infused into real butter over eight hours. Never distillate-only.

03

Small-batch baking

Batches of 60 or fewer, mixed by hand, so every gram of dough is evenly dosed.

04

Verified & sealed

Independent lab results on every label, then sealed in child-resistant, discreet packaging.
